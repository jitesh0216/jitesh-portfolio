FestOS — Festival Operations Control Portal

A unified command and operations portal designed to eliminate administrative fragmentation during mega-scale university cultural festivals.

2,836 Delegates Coordinated
38 University Contingents
31 Concurrent Competitions
1,450 Beds & Logistics

The Operational Dilemma: College festivals fail not from lack of talent, but from logistics disintegration. When 38 outside delegations arrive simultaneously across 9 venues, spreadsheet-based coordination causes catastrophic bottlenecks in accommodation, judge scheduling, and vendor handovers.

Engineered Solution: FestOS delivers a single pane of glass for 48 organizers operating across 3 shifts over 4 intense days. Modules include real-time bed inventory tracking, conflict-free venue slotting, and instantaneous vendor token dispatch.

The Agronomic Challenge: Smallholder farmers in India frequently suffer severe crop loss due to delayed disease identification and erratic water availability. Most commercial solutions require expensive broadband connectivity and high-end smartphones that do not function in remote fields.

PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE CRYPTOGRAPHIC SPEC PROTOTYPE SPEC

TRUSTMESH — Proof-of-Reality Protocol

“Trust the event. Not the identity.” A decentralized verification framework for attesting to real-world occurrences without compromising personal identity.

ZERO PII Identity Exposure
REACT NAT Expo Mobile Node
FLOOD-DIS Disaster Scenario Tested
TS ADMIN TypeScript Web Portal

The Privacy Conundrum: In disaster relief or emergency reporting (e.g. municipal floods, accidents, humanitarian deliveries), verifying that an incident occurred currently requires citizens to surrender identity numbers, phone records, and location histories—deterring truthful reporting and creating massive surveillance risks.

Engineered Solution: TRUSTMESH decouples event attestation from biometric identity. Mobile nodes generate cryptographically signed environmental proofs verified by a consensus of nearby peer devices and municipal admin consoles, upholding absolute witness privacy.

React Native Expo TypeScript Zero-Knowledge Attestation
DISPATCH NO. 04
FINTECH & SAAS CROSS-BORDER RAILS FINANCIAL ENGINE SPEC

Exchequer / ApexTreasury

Automated multi-currency treasury infrastructure for Indian technology exporters receiving foreign revenue, featuring EEFC automated ring-fencing.

SUPABASE Edge Functions
HMAC-256 Webhook Authentication
POSTGRES Row-Level Security
RBI-EEFC Regulatory Compliance

The Financial Drain: Indian SaaS and software exporters lose 2% to 4.5% on forced auto-conversions when receiving USD/EUR wires. Traditional banks fail to automate transfers into Exchange Earners’ Foreign Currency (EEFC) accounts, resulting in steep currency conversion slippage.

Engineered Solution: An event-driven treasury layer utilizing Supabase Edge Functions, HMAC-SHA256 authenticated gateway webhooks, and granular PostgreSQL RLS. Inflows are dynamically ring-fenced to fund foreign-currency SaaS expenses before INR conversion.

Supabase PostgreSQL RLS HMAC-SHA256 EEFC Treasury
DISPATCH NO. 05
DISTRIBUTED PROTOCOL NETWORK RESILIENCE PROTOCOL EXPLORATION

OfflinePay — Payment-Continuity Architecture

A resilient cryptographic fallback payment protocol enabling peer-to-peer commerce when cellular infrastructure collapses or in remote connectivity deserts.

BLE / QR Physical Mesh Channels
REPLAY Cryptographic Defense
BOUNDED Double-Spend Caps
SETTLE Post-Online Reconciliation

The Connectivity Failure: Digital payment rails in India process billions of transactions, but fail instantly during network outages, natural calamities, or in high-density festival grounds where base stations become overwhelmed.

Engineered Solution: OfflinePay provisions bounded offline credit balances using asymmetric cryptographic signatures exchanged via dynamic QR codes and Bluetooth Low Energy. Replay protection buffers and eventual ledger reconciliation ensure zero transaction loss upon network reconnection.

Asymmetric Cryptography Dynamic QR BLE Transport Ledger Reconciliation
